Privacy Policy - Joyeuse VM Lane

Joyeuse VM Lane is a personal, single-user tool. Its only user is its owner.

What it accesses: the owner's own Google Drive, Gmail, and Calendar data, through Google's APIs, only when the owner directs it to.

Where data goes: data is processed on the owner's own virtual machine and hardware. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Retention: data remains under the owner's control on his own systems and can be deleted by him at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
